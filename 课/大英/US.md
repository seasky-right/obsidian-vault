## Unit 8 American Government

### Three branches of the government

- The Constitution（美国宪法）
	- framed in Philadelphia in 1787.
	- the oldest written constitution in the world.

- federalism 联邦制
- separation of powers 三权分立
- checks and balances 制衡机制

- Legislative branch
	- Congress
		- Senate 参议院
			- 100 senators in total
			- 2 senators from each state
			- Term: 6 years
			- 1/3 elected every two years
			- A senator must:
				- be over 30 years old
				- be an American citizen for 9 years
			- The Vice President is the president of the Senate.

		- House of Representatives 众议院
			- 435 representatives
			- elected every 2 years
			- number based on state population
			- A representative must:
				- be at least 25 years old
				- be an American citizen for 7 years

		- Main Functions: 
			- make laws
			- declare war
			- collect taxes
			- approve the national budget

- Executive Branch
	- The President
		- head of state
		- head of government
		- commander-in-chief of armed forces（武装部队总司令）
		- Presidential Term: 4 years
		- Presidential Powers
			1. execute laws
			2. make treaties
			3. appoint judges and ambassadors
			4. transmit the budget to Congress
			5. pardon criminals（赦免罪犯）
		- Limitations: 
			- needs Congress to approve spending
			- needs Senate approval for treaties
			- can be impeached by Congress

- Judicial Branch
	- created by the Constitution
	- 1 Chief Justice + 8 Associate Justices
	- highest court in the US
	- Main Function
		- interpret the Constitution
		- declare laws unconstitutional（宣布法律违宪）
	- Importantce: The Supreme Court established itself as interpreter of the Constitution in 1803.

### Checks and balances (separation of powers)

- Core Idea: The three branches check and balance one another.
- Examples
	- President can veto bills.
	（总统可否决法案）
	- Congress can override a veto.
	（国会可推翻否决）
	- Supreme Court can declare laws unconstitutional.
	（最高法院可宣布法律违宪）

### US constitution and Bill of Rights

- first ten amendments to the Constitution
- ratified in 1791

- Purpose: Protect personal liberties and prevent government abuse.

### Two party system and US presidential election

- total electors: 538
- 435 Representatives

- 3 from Washington D.C.
- Winner-Take-All Principle
